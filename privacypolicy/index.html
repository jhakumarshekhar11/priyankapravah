This Policy explains how Priyanka Pravah collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data, in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India.

1. Who We Are

Priyanka Pravah is a literary and poetry platform where users can read, share, and discuss creative writing. For any privacy-related queries, you can reach us at priyanka.rumu23@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: name, username, email address, and password (stored in encrypted/hashed form) when you register for an account.
  • Profile information: any bio, profile picture, or other details you choose to add.
  • Content you post: poems, articles, comments, and any other content you submit or publish on the Site.
  • Correspondence: information you provide when you contact us directly (e.g., via email).

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent, links clicked, and general interaction with the Site.
  • Device and technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: used for login sessions, preferences, and (where enabled) analytics or advertising.

2.3 Information From Third Parties

If you choose to sign up or log in using a third-party service (e.g., Google), we may receive basic profile information (such as your name and email) from that service, subject to your privacy settings with that provider.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Allow you to post, edit, and manage comments and content.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site's features and performance.
  • Communicate with you about your account, updates, or support requests.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Once enabled, display relevant advertisements.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the DPDP Act, we process your personal data based on:

  • Your consent, given at the time of registration or when you interact with consent-based features (e.g., cookies, newsletters).
  • Legitimate use, such as operating core account and comment functionality you have voluntarily requested.
  • Legal obligation, where processing is required to comply with Indian law.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, though this may limit your ability to use certain features (such as maintaining an account).

5. User-Generated Content

Any poems, comments, or other content you post publicly on the Site are visible to other users and may be indexed by search engines. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in public posts unless you intend for it to be publicly visible. We are not responsible for how third parties use publicly posted content.

6. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

We currently use cookies for essential functions such as keeping you logged in and remembering preferences.

We plan to introduce advertising (e.g., through networks such as Google AdSense) on the Site in the future. Once active:

  • Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance.
  • These partners may collect data such as your IP address and browsing behavior on this and other sites.
  • You will be able to manage ad-related cookies through your browser settings and, where applicable, opt-out tools provided by the ad network (e.g., Google's Ads Settings).

We will update this Policy with specific details (including any analytics tools used) before or at the time advertising is enabled.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site (e.g., hosting providers, email services), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Advertising partners, once advertising is enabled.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, if required by law, court order, or government request under Indian law.
  • Successors, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their independent marketing purposes without your consent.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures (such as password hashing and access controls) to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

AccessSee the personal data we hold about you
Correct or updateFix inaccurate or incomplete data
Withdraw consentWhere consent is the basis for processing
Request erasureSubject to legal retention requirements
NominateSomeone to act on your behalf if needed
Grievance redressalEscalate to the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at priyanka.rumu23@gmail.com.

11. Children's Privacy and Minimum Age

The Site is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a user under 13 has created an account, we will take steps to delete the associated data. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal data, please contact us so we can act promptly.

Users between 13 and 18 should have the awareness or involvement of a parent/guardian when creating an account, in line with general safe-usage practice.

12. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

13. International Visitors

While this Site is operated from and governed by the laws of India, it may be accessed by users from other countries. By using the Site, you consent to your information being processed in India.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements (including when advertising is enabled). We will post the revised Policy on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of the Site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
